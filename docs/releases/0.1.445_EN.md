# Sable Life 0.1.445

## Features

- Core collision breaking detects outline-only decorations such as grass, flowers, ferns and mushrooms. Fluids are excluded; floor protection applies to actual solid support.
- Load surface / plant preset adds common vegetation and flower, sapling and leaf tags. It remains a protected list: applying it merges and deduplicates entries without changing existing world lists automatically.
- Melee gains Break blocks, Off by default. Its current box and sweep use the existing offset, extension and angle settings. It clears loaded world terrain while preserving other Sable structures, protected entries and unbreakable blocks.
- Melee uses the core's block-drop preference independently of body collision breaking, its delay and floor protection. Survival cores pay the configured melee rate per successfully removed block; failed removal refunds that charge.

## Compatibility and limits

- Older modules, presets and blueprints without the new field keep destruction Off. Existing fields, IDs and marks retain their meaning. Protocol 50, manual revision 246.
- Per-core and per-dimension work budgets can spread large areas over several ticks. Very fast sweeps can leave blocks. Disabling, release, unload and range edits discard previous sweep paths.
